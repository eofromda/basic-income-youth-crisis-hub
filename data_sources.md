# Data Sources and Methodological Notes

## Main Data Sources

This dashboard uses public statistical and policy-related sources for its basic analysis sections

- KOSIS: Korean youth unemployment and social indicators
- OECD Data: Youth unemployment rate and international comparison indicators
- Bank of Korea: Macroeconomic context, household consumption, and welfare spending discussion
- Public policy reports: Basic income experiments and youth welfare policy cases

## Case Studies

The basic income case cards are based on publicly discussed policy experiments and pilot programs

- Finland Basic Income Experiment
- Gyeonggi Youth Basic Income
- Canada Basic Income Pilot Discussions

## Simulation Notes

The simulation tab is based on a hypothetical policy scenario model

It is designed for educational exploration, not for official economic forecasting

The model estimates possible changes in

- youth unemployment pressure
- housing burden
- fiscal cost
- consumption stimulation
- welfare satisfaction

## Important Limitations

The simulation results depend on simplified assumptions

Actual policy outcomes would depend on many additional variables, including

- labour market structure
- inflation
- housing supply
- taxation design
- welfare eligibility rules
- political feasibility
- long-term fiscal sustainability

## Source Labeling

Each chart includes a short source note below the visualization

Actual indicator charts and hypothetical simulation outputs are separated clearly in the dashboard
